Global Entrepreneurship Week 2026

The future of work is changing fast, driven by artificial intelligence, automation, digital transformation and emerging technologies. Entrepreneurship is becoming a critical path to jobs, innovation, economic growth and sustainable livelihoods.

What GEW is

Global Entrepreneurship Week is a global initiative that celebrates entrepreneurship, creativity, innovation and enterprise development. It brings together entrepreneurs, young people, businesses, investors, policymakers, institutions and other stakeholders to build stronger entrepreneurial ecosystems.

Our regional commitment

As the Zonal Implementing Organization for the South-South and South-East (SS/SE), TLI Concept and Project Support Services is coordinating GEW 2026 activities across five states.

  1. Rivers
  2. Bayelsa
  3. Enugu
  4. Imo
  5. Abia

The regional initiative is coordinated in collaboration with the Enterprise Development Centre (EDC), the EDC Alumni Network Port Harcourt Hub, and the Global Entrepreneurship Network (GEN) Nigeria / GEW Nigeria ecosystem.

Why GEW 2026 matters to the future of work

  1. Entrepreneurship

    Inspiring individuals to become job creators and opportunity builders.

  2. Technology & Innovation

    Promoting AI, digital skills and technology-driven solutions.

  3. Skills Development

    Equipping individuals with creativity, adaptability, financial literacy and problem-solving skills.

  4. Collaboration

    Connecting entrepreneurs, investors, mentors and institutions to unlock opportunities.

  5. Girls and Women Empowerment

    Encouraging more girls and women to explore opportunities in technology, finance and entrepreneurship.

  6. Economic Growth

    Supporting enterprise development, job creation and sustainable livelihoods.
