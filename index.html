What we do

Three things, done properly.

Movement

Parcels, shipments and people moved on planned routes. You are told when it leaves, and when it lands. Every trip is run to a safety standard we do not cut corners on.

Fleet

Cars, vans and trucks under one roof, so whatever the job needs, we already have it. One fleet, kept serviced, built to cover every kind of logistics work you bring us.

Standard

Built on a culture that takes maintenance seriously and treats customers properly. Vehicles are inspected on a schedule, not when something breaks, and every call gets a straight answer.

Why TLI Capps

Standards we do not compromise on.

Maintenance

We inspect before something goes wrong, not after. Brakes, tyres and engines are checked against a fixed calendar, and any fault a driver flags on the road gets fixed before that vehicle is booked out again.

Planning

Routes are worked out before a wheel turns. Drivers know the road, the schedule, and the load, so timing holds even when the city does not cooperate.

Reachability

One point of contact from the first message to the last drop. No call centre, no hand-offs — the person who takes your booking is the one who sees it through.
