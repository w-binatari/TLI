About

Who we are.

TLI CAPSS is a registered social enterprise based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. We help individuals, businesses, and organizations achieve their objectives efficiently through logistics, transportation, M&E, business development, and training.

Founder and Managing Director

Mr. Emmanuel Oturu

An alumnus of the Enterprise Development Centre (EDC), Mr. Oturu leads TLI CAPSS with a focus on innovation, business growth, accountability, and quality service delivery. He is committed to developing practical solutions that create value for clients and contribute to sustainable business outcomes.
