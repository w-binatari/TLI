Who we are

One TLI, several ventures.

TLI Concept and Project Support Services, TLI CAPSS for short, is a registered social enterprise in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. It is the parent company of Errand Boy, TLI Logistics and the TLI Values, Ethics and Leadership Programme, and it provides monitoring and evaluation, business development and training in its own right.

Founder and Managing Director

Mr. Emmanuel Oturu

EDC alumnus and Zonal Coordinator, Global Entrepreneurship Week 2026 (South-South and South-East Nigeria). He leads TLI with a focus on innovation, growth, accountability, and quality service.
